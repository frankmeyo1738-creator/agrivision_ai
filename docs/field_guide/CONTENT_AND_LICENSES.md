## Images and rights

All seven bundled SVG illustrations in `frontend/assets/field_guide/art/` are original code-generated vector artwork created for this project, dedicated under CC0 1.0. They represent crops or a field decoratively; they are not symptom photographs or validated identification aids. Each article records its asset path, alt text, creator, rights and source. No third-party photographs, Wikipedia content, dataset images or network fonts are bundled.

Publisher references retain their own rights; a citation does not make a photograph reusable. Live RSS uses only eight publisher headline/date/link records, with attribution and direct links, not full article bodies, summaries, embedded images or scripts. No open-content license is asserted for RSS. The publisher may change or remove its public feed.
